EVENT RULES

Participate individually. Keep your login details private. Round 1 answers lock when submitted or when the slot ends. Finalists are selected by Round 1 score; ties at the cutoff qualify. Speed does not add points in Round 2.

Monitoring notice: During both rounds, this page records browser tab visibility changes, window-focus changes and fullscreen exits with timestamps for admin review. A first event displays a warning; repeated events are flagged for review. In Round 1, a second event locks the remaining questions pending admin review. In Round 2, the attempt continues while the event is flagged for review. Websites cannot reliably detect OS-level split-screen, another monitor or a second device. We do not record your screen or browsing history.
